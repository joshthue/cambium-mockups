Where the numbers come from.  Pipeline = open opportunities in your Projects, each with a potential revenue and a confidence %. Actual (Paid) = orders where the commission has been collected — our one fully reliable realized signal; zero‑commission orders are samples and are excluded. Forecast = Actual + Weighted pipeline; mark a project Won and it moves from pipeline into Actual.

The Invoiced step is manual — on purpose.  Nothing auto‑tells us a supplier has invoiced, and that's the event Brad cares about: it starts the clock toward the supplier being paid and Gehrke collecting commission — the process the team "bird‑dogs." So "Invoiced" is a milestone a rep flags on the order (with a date), giving everyone a live awaiting‑payment list to chase, instead of guessing from a legacy invoice date the old system never filled in.
