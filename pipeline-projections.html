Where the numbers come from.  Pipeline = open opportunities in Projects, each with a potential revenue and a confidence %. Actual (Paid) = orders where the commission has been collected — the one fully reliable realized signal; zero‑commission orders are samples and are excluded. Forecast = Actual + Weighted pipeline; mark a project Won and it moves from pipeline into Actual.

Scope is the me/everyone control used across Cambium (the samples "Just me" pattern). My pipeline = your projects; Everyone = the whole Gehrke pipeline, with the Rep dropdown to narrow to one person. Open build decision: is Everyone / the Rep dropdown open to all users, or gated to managers & operators (like the brokerage switcher)?

The Invoiced step is manual — on purpose. Nothing auto‑tells us a supplier has invoiced, and that's the event the team cares about: it starts the clock toward the supplier being paid and Gehrke collecting commission — the process the team "bird‑dogs." So "Invoiced" is a milestone a rep flags (with a date), giving everyone a live awaiting‑payment list to chase.  Numbers here are illustrative — this is a clickable mock.
