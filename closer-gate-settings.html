CONCEPT

Closer gate

Set when the closer enters a project. Each project gets a 0–100 readiness score from the inputs below; when it clears the threshold, the closer is notified and pinned to the project. Tune the weights to match how your team sells.

Score inputs

How much each signal contributes to a project's readiness. Weights total 100.

Deal sizeest. revenue / commission
30 / 100
Scope claritylbs, $, timing, duration filled
25 / 100
Momentumrecency + cadence of activity
20 / 100
Sample progressionapproved vs still-out
15 / 100
Aging in stagestalled = bring closer in
10 / 100
Weights total100 / 100

Entry threshold

The closer enters once a project's score reaches this, and only from the Pre-purchase phase onward.

60
Higher = closer joins later, only on the strongest deals. Lower = earlier, on more projects. Recommended start: 60.
2
projects would be ready for a closer right now at this threshold, out of 17 in Pre-purchase or later.

Overrides

Either of these pulls the closer in regardless of score.

Owner can request the closer
A rep hits "I need help closing this" on any project.
Manager can assign the closer
A manager attaches the closer to a project directly.

Who closes

The closer notified when a project clears the gate. One today; roles are per-brokerage later.

DF
Concept mockup for discussion — weights and threshold are a starting point, not a committed algorithm. The score is a weighted blend of the five inputs; the "ready right now" preview is illustrative. Deal size is the heaviest input by default, which lets a large deal pull the closer in earlier — drop its weight if momentum and readiness should always lead.
