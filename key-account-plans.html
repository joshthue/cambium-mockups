Quick note

How a plan gets built: 1. Cambium fills in the snapshot, products, projects, samples, contracts and contacts. 2. Edward drafts goals, risks and actions from that history; the rep accepts, edits or dismisses each one. 3. The rep adds what only they know (relationships, strategy). 4. Actions land on the owner's Home when due; the plan is reviewed on a set rhythm and every change is kept in its history. Mockup with fictional data.
