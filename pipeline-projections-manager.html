How the two scopes work.  Scope is the same me/everyone control used across Cambium (the samples "Just me" pattern): My pipeline = only your projects; Everyone = the whole Gehrke pipeline. The Rep dropdown appears only in Everyone mode and lets a manager or principal narrow to one person — or leave it on All reps for the company forecast. Group by → By Rep gives the per-rep leaderboard off the same data.

Open decision for the build.  Should Everyone / the Rep dropdown be available to every user, or gated to operators and managers (the way the brokerage switcher is)? That's the one fork that changes the build — everything else is shared with the rep view.

Numbers are illustrative. Same data model as the rep view: Actual = commission collected, samples excluded, Weighted = potential × confidence, Forecast = Actual + Weighted.
