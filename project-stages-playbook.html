Projects — Stages & Playbook

Turning the sampling book into a pipeline you can run, coach, and report on.
Draft for review
Oct 19, 2026

Two things make a project a pipeline rather than a pile of samples: named stages every project moves through, and a short, written rule for when it moves — so the team knows what to do and managers can oversee and report on it. The fields already exist in Cambium (status, estimate, owner); this is the pipeline that goes on top.

The stages

StageWhat it meansMove it forward when…
NewOpportunity identified; project created, named, owner assigned.The first sample is requested or sent → Sampling.
SamplingSamples are out; waiting on the customer's evaluation.The customer is engaged and talking price, volume, or timing → Negotiation.
NegotiationThey like it; commercial terms are on the table.They buy → Won. It dies → Lost (capture a reason).
WonConverted to an actual order — real business.Terminal. This is what "converted" counts.
LostCustomer passed, or it went cold with no path back.Terminal. Capture why — it powers win/loss reporting.
NurtureReal but not now — too small or wrong timing. Kept warm.It heats up → back to Sampling or Negotiation.

New › Sampling › Negotiation › Won  ·  off-ramps: Lost & Nurture  ·  every stage change leaves a one-line reason — that note is the oversight trail.

What this unlocks

  • Projects in Negotiation — the active-deal list, one filter.
  • Projects $500K+ — filter on the estimate (empty until estimates get entered — the habit to start).
  • Projects using blueberry — works today; 39 already have blueberry samples.
  • Pipeline funnel — count & $ by stage, conversion Sampling → Won.
  • Stalled & win/loss — what's gone quiet, and why we win or lose.

A few questions for you

  1. Are these six the right stages — or simpler/finer?
  2. Do you want Nurture, or is "too small" just Lost with a reason?
  3. Who owns a project — always a Gehrke rep, or can it be a broker?
  4. Is $500K the real line for a "big" project, or an example?
  5. Require a quick reason on every stage change? (Recommended — it's what makes oversight real.)
