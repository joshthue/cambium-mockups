One contract, one item, as many prices as GMI negotiated. Orders draw against the Open tier — the earliest price you haven't closed. When GMI moves to the next price, you close the current line by hand — even with a balance left — and the next tier becomes current. Closed lines stay on the contract (never deleted) so the history is intact. When an EDI order comes in, Cambium applies the price on the PO and ties it to the open tier. If every tier is closed, an inbound 850 still comes in at the price on GMI's PO — it just isn't tied to a contract price here, and Cambium flags it so you can open or add a tier; nothing is ever blocked or dropped. For this first version closing is manual; having Cambium advance automatically once a tier's quantity is used up can be added later.
